Aspiration, meet achievement.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the mentorship last?

Matches typically run for one academic year, with the option to continue if both mentor and mentee want to.

How often do mentors and mentees meet?

We recommend at least once a month, though many pairs meet more often — in person, by phone, or by video call.

Is there a cost to join?

No — the mentorship program is completely free for both mentors and mentees.
