How to read these

Every amino acid has the same backbone: an amino group (H₃N⁺), a central α-carbon with an H, and a carboxylate (COO⁻). The only thing that changes is the side chain, the shaded part. Learn the side chain and you know the amino acid.

Tricky 1-letter codes

  • F Phenylalanine: sounds like "Fenylalanine"
  • W Tryptophan: its double ring looks like a W, "tWyptophan"
  • Y Tyrosine: "tYrosine"
  • K Lysine: L was taken, K sits right before L
  • R Arginine: "aRginine", sounds like the letter R
  • D Aspartic acid: "asparDic", D comes before E like Asp before Glu
  • E Glutamic acid: "gluEtamic"
  • N Asparagine: "asparagiNe", the amide one has N
  • Q Glutamine: "Q-tamine"
  • The other 11 use their first letter: G A V L I M P S T C H
