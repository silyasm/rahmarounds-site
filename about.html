Welcome to Rahma Rounds!

Have you ever felt overwhelmed by the challenges of pursuing a medical career? At Rahma Rounds, we're here to empower young Muslim students by providing tailored opportunities and resources that strengthen your dedication and guide you every step of the way. We focus on leveling the playing field for first-generation and low-income students, ensuring they have the tools they need to succeed. Our support extends to undergraduates, medical students, residents, and doctors, fostering a strong sense of community and integrating Islamic values into the medical field.

Your medical journey is unique, and so is the support you need. Whether you're just considering a career in medicine or you're well on your way, Rahma Rounds is here to walk with you. We're more than just a support system — we're a community where your goals are nurtured, your challenges are shared, and your success is celebrated.

Our Mission

Rahma Rounds is an initiative dedicated to empowering young Muslim students in the medical field by providing tailored mentorship, resources, and support. We focus on leveling the playing field for first-generation and low-socioeconomic students, helping them navigate the complexities of a medical career. By bridging Islamic teachings with modern medicine, we foster a community that integrates faith with professional growth, offering guidance from undergraduate studies through medical school and beyond. Our mission is to create a nurturing environment where individuals from all backgrounds can thrive and make a meaningful impact in healthcare.

We're here for:

  • Undergraduate students dreaming of a future in medicine
  • Medical students seeking practical advice on navigating coursework, exams, and clinical rotations
  • Residents striving for mentorship on career advancement and work-life balance
  • Doctors wanting to connect with a community that shares their faith and professional goals
